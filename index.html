About

Claude Shannon’s statue (left) and Linglong Meng (right), photographed in front of the EECS Building on the University of Michigan’s North Campus in May 2026.

I am an undergraduate student in the Honors Mathematics program at the University of Michigan. I am very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Laura Balzano in the SPADA Lab, and Prof. Gabriel Poesia in the Poetry Lab, both in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.

My research interests broadly lie in deep learning theory and modern optimization problems, with a particular focus on the mathematical foundations of generative models and low-rank structures in large language models.

These interests grew out of my work with classical sparse coding methods. I hope to use modern mathematical perspectives to understand the internal mechanisms of learning systems and translate those insights—through training dynamics, optimization strategies, and representation structure—into the design of next-generation model architectures that are more efficient, capable, and interpretable.
