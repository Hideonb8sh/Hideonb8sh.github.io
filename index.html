About

Linglong Meng

Hi! My name is Linglong Meng, and my Chinese name is “孟令茏”. In Chinese, Linglong conveys intelligence and a quick, agile mind.

I am in the Honors Program with a major in Mathematics at the University of Michigan. I am very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Laura Balzano in the SPADA Lab and Prof. Gabriel Poesia in the Poetry Lab, both from the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.

Research

My research interests grew out of my early work with classical sparse coding methods in machine learning. I hope to use modern mathematical perspectives to understand the internal mechanisms of learning systems and translate those insights into the design of future model architectures through a deeper understanding of training dynamics, optimization strategies, and representation structure. My goal is to develop models that are more efficient, capable, and interpretable.

Recently, my research has focused on three main directions:

  1. Dynamics

    Dynamics in training of deep neural networks, and some modern optimization problems.

  2. Representations

    Both sparse representations like classical dictionary learning and transform learning, and deep representation learning.

  3. Reasonings

    Especially how language models learn, and form the way to do reasonings.
